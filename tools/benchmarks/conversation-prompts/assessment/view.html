What is scored

Three clear demonstrations, three partial attempts, three absent-skill cases and three partner-only controls. Only explicitly listed reference targets are scored except all-absent controls. Partial labels are provisional; inspect those separately. No independently adjudicated human labels or calibration claim.

The nine-positive-target case has a native reporting ceiling of 4/9 even with perfect prioritization. Its coverage deficit is exposed separately. Context position varies serialization order while preserving meaning and ownership.

Known limitations

Spanish only; 12 authored cases, not a representative learner population. No population-level confidence intervals or significance claims are made in this exploratory pilot. LLM temperatures are 0.7. Native produces quotes and explanations; the dense LLM returns labels only; Jev also returns probabilities. Output-work differences are part of the end-to-end cost/speed comparison. Failed calls are excluded from quality and latency summaries; known billed cost is retained. Rejected item content was not retained by the runner, so failed-call inspection shows validation reasons and retained valid items, not a full raw response.
