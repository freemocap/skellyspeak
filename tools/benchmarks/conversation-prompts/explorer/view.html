Length and complexity separation

The rows are requested difficulty levels. Look for short, mostly non-overlapping word-count bands and a gradual increase in language demands. Word and sentence length are surface proxies, not reliable CEFR scores.

Embedding neighborhoods in 2D and 3D

Drag to lasso in 2D; rotate and click points in 3D. Identical texts share coordinates. Cluster −1 means unassigned noise. Cluster IDs have no ordering.

Each point is one response. Nearby points have similar embedding patterns; inspect them to discover shared situations, subjects or phrasing. Identical responses overlap.

The axes are not difficulty, variety or quality. PCA preserves variance; t-SNE and UMAP emphasize local neighborhoods. Projection axes have no linguistic labels. A wide-looking cloud is not a winning prompt.

Use the full-dimensional cosine tables to compare diversity. Click or drag on the map to read a cluster, then use “Find similar” for neighbors in the original embedding space.

UMAP and t-SNE emphasize local neighborhoods. Island spacing, size and apparent gaps are not reliable measures of semantic distance. Compare parameter settings and seeds. The maps fit unique texts so repeated generations do not manufacture density; original-space HDBSCAN labels use the same unique texts. The PCA variance above describes the original observation-weighted reference, not these unique-text fits.
