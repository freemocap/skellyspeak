SkellySpeak · design system · architecture pass

One workspace, three modes, five laws.

The app is not short of components — it is short of agreements. Five surface elevations compete, ten type sizes carry unclear roles, density changes between neighbours, and depth hides behind arbitrary disclosure. This pass fixes the bones: the spine every screen obeys, and the five laws that make adding lessons, coaching layers and audio inspection cheap instead of another special case.

01 · the spine

Practice · Learn · Review — three places, one chrome

Today a lesson is a dialog, analysis is a pane, evidence is an overlay, and audio inspection is another dialog — each inventing its own relationship to the conversation. Name three modes instead. Each is the same three-zone frame, so a new depth layer has an obvious home rather than a new modal.

The frame every mode fills
Rail What you can switch to. Collapsible, never modal.
Main · sheet The thing you are doing, on the one raised surface. Reading lives here.
Study · inset What the app noticed. Coaching, evidence, explanation. Always this well, in every mode.

Narrow windows keep the same three zones as one column with a bottom mode bar: rail becomes a drawer, main and study become two swipeable surfaces. Nothing is invented for mobile.

04 · law three

Two densities

Chrome is compact and consistent; content is comfortable and consistent. Nothing in between, and no component decides for itself.

Chrome density Pane headers, control strips, rails, mode bar. Row height 40. Interior 12. Gap 8. Control height 32 (44 on touch).
Content density Messages, lessons, evidence, explanation. Interior 16. Block gap 16. Section gap 24. Bubble padding 12 / 16.
Ramp: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 40. No 6, 10, 14, 18, 20, 32.

06 · law five

Depth has three shapes, and only three

Suggestions collapse, coaching collapses, the coach dock collapses, XP records collapse inside a collapsed coach entry, and a lesson lives behind a dialog containing its own disclosures. The rule: one level of collapse per pane, and the shape is decided by the content's relationship to the exchange — not by convenience.

Inline · beside the words Belongs to one word or one message: glosses, translation, a correction chip. Expands in flow, closes on its own. Never more than one open per message.
Study pane · beside the exchange Belongs to the conversation: coaching, analysis, XP records, lesson recap. The pane swaps content; it does not stack collapses. This is where the lesson now lives.
Dialog · needs the whole window Only for inspection that cannot share space: recording inspector, YAML preview, settings, persona editor. Four dialogs in the app, and they say so in their title.

Asking for directions

You will be able to stop a stranger politely and ask where something is, then follow a one-sentence answer.

Spanish asks with ¿Dónde está…? for a fixed place and ¿Hay… por aquí? for any one nearby. Both take the definite article when the place is known to you both.

Examples
¿Dónde está la estación de tren? dón·de es·tá la es·ta·ción de tren Where is the train station?
¿Hay una farmacia por aquí? ai u·na far·ma·cia por a·quí Is there a pharmacy near here?

13 · adopting it

What this costs in the codebase

The laws are mostly deletions, which is why they make future features cheaper. Suggested order — each step is shippable alone.

1 · tokens.cssAlias --card/--dock/--quiet/--chip to the three surface roles and the six type roles to the scale. No component changes; the whole app gets quieter in one commit.
2 · one pane headerA single header rule shared by .chat-head, .break-head, .coach-thread-head and .panel-tabs. Deletes the tab-group chrome and the 26px control strip.
3 · fold the dockThe coach dock becomes a state of the study pane: drop the resizer, the collapsed variants and the dialog copy of the dock. The pane divider stays the only draggable edge.
4 · lesson as a modeRoute LessonDialog into the main pane with the chooser in the rail and the step spine above it. The handoff to chat becomes a mode switch, not a dialog close.
5 · colour auditRepoint --accent* to the single interaction ink and leave the domain tokens to evidence. Amber only where a correction exists; red only where a request failed.

Resolved since: Review keeps both readings of the evidence (tree above, bars below, section 10). Contacts is renamed Partners and stays a rail. Sections 14–18 extend the same frame to the empty state, lesson creation, the persona editor, mystery partners and Settings — grounded in the current AI-access, Reading and lesson-chooser contracts, not invented.

14 · empty conversation

The three ways in

The main entry point most people hit most often. The composer is always the first path — it never moves, empty state or not. Before any message exists, two more paths sit under one "or" above it: let the partner take the first turn (one tap, dealer's choice — a topic is a secondary link, never a required step), or take a lesson. This matches the real contract: one "You start" button beside an optional topic dropdown defaulting to "Any topic," with Take a lesson alongside it — restyled, not reinvented.

17 · mystery partner, in practice

Guess before you're told

A Persona tab joins Coaching and Evidence in the study well: locked rows instead of prose. Guessing is always available, and the app nudges it after enough turns. A correct guess pays XP the moment you pick it — revealing the fact on the sheet is a separate, deliberate tap, so a correct guess doesn't spoil the read for you if you'd rather keep guessing blind.
