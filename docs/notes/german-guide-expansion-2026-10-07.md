## Assessment alignment, read-only

Read the guidance in all eight existing German assessment documents and compared it with taught constructions. No concrete contradictory meaning or exclusion of a valid taught form was found. In particular: modal negation distinguishes absence of necessity from prohibition; polite requests are not labelled ability; present-tense future arrangements remain valid; running within a garden uses a location while running into it uses a destination; acknowledgments do not imply agreement; time answers can be fragments; reasons and sequence are distinguished.

The guides teach ordinary written weil clauses with final finite verbs. The assessment explicitly allows contextually appropriate conversational weil with main-clause order. This broader assessment allowance does not contradict the construction taught here, and no duplicate assessment change is proposed.
