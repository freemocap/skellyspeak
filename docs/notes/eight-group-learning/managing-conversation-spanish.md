## Learner guide

Establish contact, coordinate turns and repair mutual understanding.

### Opening and closing

Establish or end a conversational encounter.

Use a greeting or farewell as part of the actual encounter, rather than quoting it as a word.

> Hasta luego.

See you later.

This closes an encounter; repeating it in a vocabulary exercise does not.

### Acknowledging and responding

Register receipt, attention or a socially relevant response.

Show that you received a contribution or respond to a social act. Entiendo need not mean agreement.

> Gracias por avisarme.

Thank you for letting me know.

The response acknowledges useful information and thanks the speaker.

### Asking for clarification

Signal trouble hearing or understanding a contribution and seek repair.

Ask for repetition or an explanation when you cannot follow the preceding contribution.

> ¿Qué quieres decir?

What do you mean?

The question asks the partner to explain an unclear contribution, not verify a fact they already understood.

### Rephrasing

Express a prior meaning differently to improve mutual understanding.

Quiero decir can introduce a different way to express the meaning you intended.

> Quiero decir que llego después de las ocho.

I mean that I arrive after eight.

In a repair sequence, this restates the speaker's arrival time.

### Correcting a misunderstanding

Replace an incorrect interpretation with the intended one.

Contrast the mistaken interpretation with what you meant.

> No, me refiero al martes, no al jueves.

No, I mean Tuesday, not Thursday.

The speaker corrects which day the partner understood.

### Managing turns and topics

Coordinate who contributes and what the exchange is about.

Use a transition or a turn-management expression when changing the conversation's direction.

> Por cierto, ¿cómo está tu hermana?

By the way, how is your sister?

Por cierto marks a shift to a different topic.
