## Editorial cases

Paper expectations only, with context supplied by the actual exchange. Negative cases assume no other successful use. Subfunction ambiguity does not itself make correct group use unclear.

| Group | Direct / positive | Unsuccessful or absent | Contextual | Ambiguity / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People/places | `As plantas estão secas.` | Intended ordinary description `As plantas está seco.` alone fails relevant agreement. | `Perto da praça.` after asking the library's location. | `Sua bicicleta` requires an owner/referent from context; seu does not uniquely identify one owner. |
| Time/events | `Ontem troquei o pneu.` | Intended ordinary past report `Ontem eu trocar.` alone fails. | `Ontem à noite.` after asking when the learner arrived. | `há seis meses` can mean continuing duration or ago depending on the main event. |
| Information | `A que horas a biblioteca abre?` | A copied question word without an intelligible question. | `Às oito.` after asking the opening time. | `Às oito?` may check a fact or repair hearing. |
| Viewpoints | `Gosto de cozinhar.` | Intended liking `Eu gosto cozinhar.` alone lacks de. | `Sim.` after an identified evaluative position can align. | `Entendi.` may show understanding without agreement; `Concordo.` explicitly expresses agreement despite omitting its object. |
| Possibilities | `É possível que chova amanhã.` | Intended requirement `Eu tenho devolver.` alone lacks que/de. | `Sim.` from the responsible person after asking permission to use the table. | `Posso usar esta mesa?` can ask permission or opportunity; both can establish the group. A permission-giver typically answers `Pode.`, not first-person `Posso.` to that same question. |
| Connections | `Fiquei em casa porque estava chovendo.` | Isolated broken porque without a reason or another correct relation. | `Porque estava chovendo.` after asking why the learner stayed home. | `Não sei se está aberta.` is an indirect whether question, not a condition. |
| Action | `Você pode guardar estes livros, por favor?` | A copied por favor without any recoverable requested action. | `Sim.` after an invitation can accept. | `Eu levo as caixas amanhã.` undertakes responsibility in planning; elsewhere it may simply report an event. |
| Conversation | `Eu quis dizer sábado, não sexta.` repairs an interpretation. | Quoting olá as vocabulary without opening contact. | `Sim.` after an explanation can register receipt. | `Pode repetir?` can repair hearing; `Posso falar dos horários agora?` manages a topical turn, rather than automatically claiming speech ability. |
