Irish patterns are taught on their own terms: copular identification and preference; verb-first ordinary statements; prepositional pronouns for possession, emotion and permission; question and dependent bí forms; verbal-noun objects and infinitive order; lenition/eclipsis in the actual examples; habitual versus ongoing activity; necessity versus ordinary future; open conditions and focused corrections.
