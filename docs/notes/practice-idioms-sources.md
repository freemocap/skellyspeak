# Idiomatic practice phrase sources

Status: authored content implemented, 2026-09-30; speaker review pending.

Each of the 20 default banks now contains eight idiomatic phrases. The existing
Modern Standard Arabic replacement bank also contains eight, for 168 additional
entries. These are conventional idioms and sayings, sometimes inflected into
short practice utterances. They are not a sixth difficulty level. They load into
the normal candidate picker and require explicit Keep, just like the other sets.

The sources below were checked online for expression forms and meanings. They
include dictionaries, teaching resources and community collections; this is not
a frequency study or proof that every expression is equally usual in every
variety. Search excerpts were sufficient for the Mandarin and Indonesian
collections; the other main collections were opened. New sentence framing was
authored locally. No source's explanatory prose or example narrative was copied.
All banks retain `needs_review`. In particular, idiom register, gendered forms,
regional familiarity and naturally spoken phrasing still need speaker review.

## Authoring maintenance

New languages and complete variety replacement banks must provide `idiomatic`
alongside the other five sets. Native validation requires 8–50 nonempty distinct
phrases per set; structural checks do not promote linguistic review status.
Keep the original authored spelling. Source corrections belong in the data and
this ledger, without adding language-specific executable logic.
