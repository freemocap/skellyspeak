06 / REVIEW NOTES

Decide the basics before migrating.

Start with palette and typography, then controls. We can change this guide before touching the app.

  1. Surfaces: cool neutral or warm; restrained borders; overlay-only shadows.
  2. Typography: sans UI, serif reading, mono metadata; role-based sizes.
  3. Controls: standard or compact desktop; shared states and focus treatment.
  4. Conversation: partner tint, word-help density and reading/input scale.

Settings reset when you reload. Nothing is stored or transmitted. Tell me section numbers and what feels right or wrong.
