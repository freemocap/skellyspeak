## Research boundary

ACTFL's Can-Do overview describes progression in function, context and text type.
It emphasizes consistency across situations, explicitly rejects one-time checklist
completion, and says its examples are not a prescribed curriculum.
[@actfl_cando_structure2026]

Robinson distinguishes task complexity, learner-dependent difficulty and
interaction conditions; only the chapter summary was reviewed here.
[@robinson_task_complexity2001]

These support observable capabilities, increasing demands and accumulated
contextual evidence. They do not establish four, five or six semantic primitives,
a learning prerequisite DAG, equal branch counts, or a mapping of our stages to
CEFR/ACTFL levels. CEFR activity modes (reception, production, interaction,
mediation) are a different classification from the meaning-making functions
being proposed here. Existing broad sources remain background, not validation.

## Proposed starting point: four families

Each family has an observable entry descriptor. Family membership is navigation;
a claim of competence concerns a specific descriptor and evidence, not a whole
family merely because its first example was observed.

| Family | Entry / zero | Beginner branches | Intermediate refinements |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identify | Point something out | Specify things; locate things | Track referents; shift viewpoint |
| Describe | Express a state/event | Who does what; place events in time | Explain event relations; sequence events |
| Qualify | Affirm or negate | Distinguish alternatives; express wants/limits | Control scope; reason or condition |
| Interact | Make a social move | Ask and answer; coordinate with someone | Report perspectives; clarify and repair |

This is 4 + 8 + 8 = **20 provisional descriptors**, not an adopted 20-skill
replacement. Several labels bundle distinctions in the current catalog. A
crosswalk must expose those distinctions rather than hide the existing 45 inside
20 vague headings. Clarification/repair is a proposed addition, not an existing
named skill. Intermediate reporting can combine interaction and reference;
conditionals can combine qualification and event-time meaning.

Four keeps the overview small. Five may be clearer if **Connect ideas** becomes
its own family: reasons, conditions, concession and reports currently crowd
Qualify/Interact. A fifth family needs an independently meaningful entry
capability, not a root that only makes sense at intermediate level. Six should
need evidence of a boundary problem that neither four nor five can solve.

## Graph rules

- A solid edge means a more demanding/refined use, not a required learning order.
- A dotted edge means another capability contributes to a combined task.
- Refinement/combination edges point toward later complexity stages in this
  sketch, preserving acyclicity. Symmetric relatedness links belong elsewhere.
- Do not lock a capability because another has not been observed. Learners may
  use formulaic advanced constructions before flexible control of simpler ones.
- A combined capability is one node with multiple incoming edges, not one copy
  under each parent. Do not create nodes for all possible combinations.
- Family summaries aggregate evidence; they do not independently award the same
  attempt again. Advanced evidence does not automatically mark all ancestors
  independently demonstrated.

## Branching budget

For R entry nodes, B beginner branches per entry, and I intermediate branches
per beginner, a regular tree contains R + R×B + R×B×I nodes. Thus 4 + 8 + 24 = 36,
not 32. Five roots under the same 2×3 expansion produce 45; six produce 54.
A further factor of two at the next stage makes the four-root version 84 nodes.

The proposed 20-descriptor sketch limits intermediate expansion. Real branching
should be irregular and driven by useful distinctions. Do not force every node
to have the same number of children merely for visual symmetry. The equal counts
here are a comparison device, not an accepted ontology.

Maintenance cost includes each node's criteria, examples/near misses, language
cores, variety sections and evaluation fixtures. A repeated combination costs
more than an extra edge. Moving distinctions into unnamed subcriteria does not
actually remove that work; review the full content surface before claiming savings.

## Complexity and learner evidence

“Entry / zero” means a first meaningful use, often one word or a conventional
chunk with contextual support. Beginner means simple self-composed relations.
Intermediate means connected relations, controlled scope or maintained viewpoint.
These are provisional product descriptions, not CEFR certificates or ACTFL ranks.

Track actual attempts, assistance, revision, observed success/uncertainty and
context separately. One utterance can inform multiple capabilities when it
contains direct evidence for each. An unobserved node means unknown, not failure.
The visual example (“If it rains, can we meet at your house tomorrow?”) is an
English gloss illustrating evidence placement, not a model judgment or learner
record. Reading/listening ability cannot be inferred from producing such text.

Advanced work can initially deepen the same capabilities: sustained discourse,
nuance, register, independence and unfamiliar contexts. Deferring advanced
content is a scope choice; intermediate ability is not completion of language
learning. Keep an extensible boundary without authoring another branching tier.
