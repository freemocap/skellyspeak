# Refer to the past

Time and events

Express that a situation or event occurred before now or an established reference time.

**Boundary:** Judge the expressed time relationship, not the verb form alone.

## Meaning

` before(time(event), reference_time) `

A completed event happened before now or another time being discussed.

- ` event ` — The completed event being described.
- ` time(event) ` — When it happened.
- ` reference_time ` — Now or another time established in the conversation.

## Examples

### 1. English

> Ana arrived yesterday.

` before(time(arrival), now) `

The event is located before the current day in the ordinary reading.

### 2. English

> I was reading when Ana arrived.

Reading was in progress when Ana arrived. The simple formula above does not capture this overlap.

### 3. English

> By next Friday, I will have finished.

Finishing is earlier than a future reference time; the sentence does not establish completion before now.

### 4. English

**Context:** When will you arrive?

> Tomorrow.

Places arrival in the future in this context; provides no past-reference evidence.
