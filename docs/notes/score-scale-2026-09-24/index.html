Repeatability, time and cost

Normalized distance uses (score−1)/4 for 1–5 and score/10 for 0–10. This is a comparison convention for ordinal ratings, not proof of equal intervals. Repeat distance excludes pairs containing abstentions or invalid answers.

Partner understanding

Evaluated in a separate request using the actual partner reply. Emotion and agreement are not measures of understanding.

Method and exact prompts

36 cases × two scales × two repetitions = 144 rating requests. Another 72 requests assess partner understanding. Rating inputs exclude the later partner response. This compares two anchored classifier scales, not the existing generative feedback endpoint.
