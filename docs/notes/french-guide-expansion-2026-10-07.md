# French guide expansion — 2026-10-07

Implemented: all 42 sections in the English and Cantonese teaching editions, eight groups, France and Canada (16 YAML files). Assessment files and shared definitions are outside this edit. Principal examples are preserved. Exercises belong in example notes; section explanations remain declarative because partner practice focus consumes them.

## Sections audited and revised

| Group | Sections | Main teaching changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| coordinating_action | requests, offers_invitations, suggestions, commitments, accepting_declining, negotiating_plans | Polite conditional, informal invitations, optional suggestions versus commitments, future inability, personal availability. |
| feelings_viewpoints | wants_intentions, preferences, emotions, opinions, agreement_disagreement | Desire versus intention, preferred choice with à, affected person with me/nous, adjective agreement, scope of agreement. |
| information_exchange | asking_information, answering_information, checking_facts, useful_detail | Open versus confirmation questions, contextual short time answer, confirmation bien, relative que versus qui and useful landmarks. |
| managing_conversation | opening_closing, acknowledging, clarification, rephrasing, correcting_misunderstanding, turns_topics | Closing expectations, understanding versus agreement, meaning versus repetition, faithful reformulation, repair and formal versus everyday turn requests. |
| people_places | identification, qualities_states, location_movement, possession_relations, quantity_comparison | C’est versus profession after être, noun agreement, au/à la, motion versus location, possession and plus/que comparison. |
| possibilities_constraints | ability, permission, obligation, possibility, certainty | Savoir versus pouvoir, contextual permission, prohibition versus absence of necessity, uncertain weather predictions and agreement in certainty claims. |
| reasons_connections | causes_reasons, consequences, conditions, contrast, supporting_claims | Reason clause versus noun reason, consequence versus sequence, open si condition and y, contrasting expectations, measured support and its limits. |
| time_events | present_events, past_events, future_events, duration_frequency, event_sequence, event_phase | Present activity versus habit, past background/event, aller plus infinitive, ongoing depuis versus completed duration, ordered past events, two nested de constructions in recent completion. |

Each section now begins with a plausible situation, explains the principal French forms, gives an adaptable construction and useful contrast, then has a short practice prompt with an answer and reason in examples.note. Both explanation editions teach equivalent constructions and practice targets. No example-string correction was needed.
