Each section opens with the conversational purpose and a plausible situation.
Principal-example meanings unpack the actual Italian words, agreement, verb forms
and preposition combinations where useful. Notes offer an adaptation or contrast
and a short practice item with an answer and the reason for its form. These are
original exercises, not assessment items or a second evaluator contract.

The section.explanation fields remain declarative because the partner-focus
consumer includes them. Exercises and commands to practise are confined to
examples.note. Inline code marks actual target expressions; Cantonese explanatory
prose and grammatical labels remain plain. No regional comparison or variety
replacement was necessary for the single declared Italian profile.

Particular teaching work covers singular-family article exceptions versus ordinary
possessive phrases, feminine/plural adjective agreement, location versus movement,
preposition/article combinations, ongoing present duration with da, programmed
future events in the present, completed versus interrupted painting, modal
infinitives and the difference between learned skill and circumstantial ability.
Commitments are grounded in a supplies-planning exchange rather than inferred from
verb tense. Understanding and agreement, and chronological sequence and logical
consequence, receive distinct explanations. The uncertainty lesson distinguishes
indirect-question se from conditional se.

## Assessment alignment review

Read all eight existing Italian assessment.yaml files without editing them.
No taught valid construction was found to be explicitly excluded, and no
contradictory lesson meaning was found. Concrete agreements are:

- People/places accepts identity, agreement, location and direction, possession,
  quantity and comparison; the lessons distinguish these without treating a
  preference as a comparative property.
- Time/events accepts suitable simple present, ongoing forms, compound past and
  other contextually appropriate past tenses. The lessons do not prescribe one
  past tense across Italy or map every ongoing event to an English progressive.
  Ongoing duration, scheduled future, ordering, completion and interruption align.
- Information/exchange makes short answers depend on their real question and
  distinguishes confirmation from open information requests. Lessons follow this.
- Coordination grounds present-tense commitments in an actual planning exchange,
  accepts indicative/conditional requests and distinguishes wanting from promising.
- Possibilities/constraints distinguishes learned skill, permission, necessity,
  practical opportunity, possible states and lack of knowledge. Modal activities
  stay infinitive; a request for another person's action is not taught as evidence
  of that person's ability.
- Feelings/viewpoints grounds disagreement in a recoverable view; opinions and
  preferences are not treated as action undertakings or bare price facts.
- Reasons/connections distinguishes condition, reason, sequence, contrast and
  grounds; observations support a judgment without guaranteeing its truth.
- Managing/conversation grounds clarification, reformulation and correction in
  the prior exchange; acknowledgment is not automatically agreement.

This was a semantic source comparison, not live assessment inference. Assessments
remain the existing separate contracts. The general reviewer still needs to check
novel alternatives and practice answers for naturalness and register.
